## Overview

Jubensha (Chinese-style scripted murder mystery games) has arrived in New York City's Chinatown and Flushing communities. While smaller than the scene in Shanghai or Shenzhen, NYC's jubensha offerings cater to the large Chinese international student and diaspora community. Scripts are primarily in Mandarin Chinese.

## Recommended Venues

### Jubensha Studios

**Murder Mystery Club NYC (Manhattan Chinatown)**
- **Address**: 88 Mott St, 3rd Floor, Manhattan (Chinatown)
- **Highlights**: One of the first dedicated jubensha studios in NYC Chinatown. Offers box scripts and city-limited scripts in Mandarin Chinese. DMs are experienced players from the Chinese jubensha community. Intimate rooms decorated to match script themes.
- **Price Range**: $35–60/person
- **Duration**: 3–5 hours
- **Transport**: J, Z to Bowery; 6 to Canal St

**ScriptKill Studio (Flushing, Queens)**
- **Address**: 136-20 Roosevelt Ave, 2nd Floor, Queens (Flushing)
- **Highlights**: Flushing's premier jubensha venue. Larger space than Chinatown locations, with more elaborately decorated rooms. Carries popular scripts including emotional, horror, and hard-core mystery genres. Popular with Chinese international students from nearby colleges (Baruch, NYU, Columbia).
- **Price Range**: $30–55/person
- **Duration**: 3–5 hours
- **Transport**: 7 to Flushing–Main St

**Manga Murder Mystery (Elmhurst, Queens)**
- **Address**: 82-02 Broadway, Queens (Elmhurst)
- **Highlights**: Neighborhood jubensha studio serving the Queens Asian community. Known for friendly DMs and a welcoming atmosphere for first-time players. Smaller script library but solid selection of popular titles. Also stocks manga and anime merch.
- **Price Range**: $28–45/person
- **Duration**: 3–4 hours
- **Transport**: M, R to Elmhurst Ave

**The Detective's Den (East Village)**
- **Address**: 34 E 1st St, Manhattan (East Village)
- **Highlights**: Caters to both Chinese-speaking and English-speaking players. Offers bilingual scripts and DMs. A bridge between the jubensha format and Western murder mystery traditions. Good for mixed-language groups. Also hosts Blood on the Clocktower nights.
- **Price Range**: $40–65/person
- **Duration**: 3–4 hours
- **Transport**: 6 to Astor Pl; F to 2nd Ave

**Jubensha Meetup NYC (Pop-up Events)**
- **Highlights**: Not a fixed venue — a community organizing pop-up jubensha sessions at rented spaces across Manhattan and Queens. Primarily organized through Meetup.com and WeChat groups. Good for players who can't commit to a fixed location. Sessions typically happen 2–3 times per month.
- **Price Range**: $25–45/person
- **Join**: Search "Jubensha NYC" on Meetup.com or WeChat

### Western Murder Mystery Alternatives

**Accomplice New York (Immersive)**
- **Address**: Various starting points in Manhattan
- **Highlights**: Not jubensha, but the closest Western equivalent — an immersive outdoor murder mystery experience. Actors guide small groups through real NYC streets to solve a crime. English.
- **Price Range**: $70–85/person
- **Duration**: 2.5–3 hours

## Tips

- Most NYC jubensha scripts are in Mandarin Chinese; confirm language when booking
- Flushing and Chinatown have the highest density of jubensha venues
- Sessions require 5–8 players; venues can help assemble groups (拼车)
- Bring snacks — sessions can last 4–5 hours
- WeChat groups are the primary way to organize games in the NYC Chinese community
- Bilingual options are expanding as the format gains crossover appeal
- Weekends book up fast; reserve 3–5 days ahead
