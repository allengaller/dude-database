## Overview

New York City is the East Coast hub for anime, manga, and pop culture merchandise. From Midtown's Book Off to the annual Anime NYC convention at the Javits Center, the city offers robust retail and community options for collectors and fans. The East Village and Chinatown also have hidden gems for imported goods.

## Recommended Venues

### Retail Stores

**Book Off**
- **Address**: 49 W 45th St, Manhattan (Midtown)
- **Highlights**: Japanese used bookstore chain with an extensive manga section (English and Japanese), anime DVDs/Blu-rays, figures, and collectibles at discounted prices. Excellent for finding out-of-print manga and rare figures. Clean, well-organized store.
- **Price Range**: Manga $5–15, Figures $20–300+, DVDs $8–25
- **Transport**: B, D, F, M to 42nd St–Bryant Park; 4, 5, 6 to Grand Central

**Kinokuniya New York**
- **Address**: 1073 Avenue of the Americas (6th Ave), Manhattan (Midtown)
- **Highlights**: The premier Japanese bookstore in NYC. Massive manga section (Japanese and English), art books, magazines (Newtype, Animage), stationery, and anime goods. Second floor has a dedicated anime/art book section. Hosts creator signings and events.
- **Price Range**: Manga $12–20, Art books $40–80, Magazines $15–25
- **Transport**: B, D, F, M to 42nd St–Bryant Park; N, Q, R to 42nd St

**Image Anime**
- **Address**: 242 W 30th St, 2nd Floor, Manhattan (Garment District)
- **Highlights**: One of NYC's oldest anime specialty shops. Packs an incredible amount of figures, model kits (Gundam, etc.), PVC statues, and plushies into a small space. Specializes in imports from Japan. Prices reflect import costs but selection is unmatched.
- **Price Range**: Figures $50–500, Model kits $25–150, Plushies $20–50
- **Transport**: A, C, E to 34th St–Penn Station; 1, 2, 3 to 34th St

**Anime Castle**
- **Address**: 213-04 26th Ave, Queens (Bayside/Whitestone)
- **Highlights**: Large anime/manga specialty store in Queens. Carries figures, manga, DVDs, posters, and cosplay accessories. Well-stocked with both new and pre-owned items. Worth the trip from Manhattan for serious collectors.
- **Price Range**: Figures $30–400, Manga $8–18, Posters $10–30
- **Transport**: QM2 bus from Midtown; or drive (parking available)

### Chinatown / Hidden Gems

**Chinatown gift shops (East Broadway / Mott St)**
- **Address**: Various shops along Mott St and East Broadway, Manhattan Chinatown
- **Highlights**: Several small shops carry affordable anime merch — phone cases, stickers, keychains, small figures, and gachapon. Quality varies but prices are low. Fun for browsing and finding cheap accessories.
- **Price Range**: Stickers $2–5, Keychains $5–10, Small figures $10–30
- **Transport**: J, Z to Bowery; 6 to Canal St

### Conventions

**Anime NYC**
- **Location**: Javits Center, 429 11th Ave, Manhattan
- **Time**: Annually in November (3 days)
- **Highlights**: The largest anime convention on the East Coast. 50,000+ attendees. Major industry booths (Crunchyroll, Aniplex, Funimation), artist alley, cosplay contests, and Japanese industry guests. Exclusive merch and premiere screenings.
- **Price Range**: $60–120 (weekend pass)
- **Transport**: 7 to Hudson Yards; A, C, E to 34th St–Penn Station

**New York Comic Con (NYCC)**
- **Location**: Javits Center
- **Time**: Annually in October (4 days)
- **Highlights**: While broader than anime (comics, gaming, film), NYCC has a massive anime/manga presence. 200,000+ attendees. Artist alley is one of the best in the country for original art and fan merch.
- **Price Range**: $70–150 (day/weekend passes)
- **Transport**: 7 to Hudson Yards

## Tips

- Kinokuniya and Book Off are within walking distance of each other — visit both in one trip
- Image Anime is a cramped but treasure-filled store; ask the owner for recommendations
- Anime NYC passes sell out months in advance — buy early
- Artist Alley at conventions is the best place for unique fan-made merch (pins, prints, stickers)
- For pre-owned figures, check Mandarake's online store (they ship from Japan)
