# New York Karaoke Guide

**Data Note**: This guide is compiled from publicly available information.

---

## Overview

New York City's karaoke scene spans two distinct worlds: stage karaoke (singing in front of strangers at a bar) and private room karaoke (renting a room with friends, Korean/Japanese-style). Koreatown (Midtown, 32nd St) is the epicenter of private room noraebang, while dive bars across Brooklyn and Manhattan host raucous open-mic karaoke nights.

## Recommended Venues

### Private Room Karaoke (Noraebang)

**Gagopa Karaoke**
- **Address**: 51 W 32nd St, 4th Floor, Manhattan (Koreatown)
- **Highlights**: A Koreatown institution. Private rooms with extensive English, Korean, Japanese, and Chinese song selections. BYOB-friendly (corkage fee). Rooms are clean and well-maintained. Open late.
- **Price Range**: $30–50/hour/room (plus corkage)
- **Hours**: 11:00 AM–4:00 AM daily
- **Transport**: B, D, F, M, N, Q, R to 34th St–Herald Sq

**Turntable LP Bar & Karaoke**
- **Address**: 137 Avenue A, Manhattan (East Village)
- **Highlights**: Hip East Village spot with vinyl records, craft cocktails, and private karaoke rooms in the back. Smaller room capacity but great vibe. Song selection skews indie/alternative but has pop hits too.
- **Price Range**: $40–60/hour/room; cocktails $14–18
- **Hours**: 5:00 PM–2:00 AM
- **Transport**: L to 1st Ave; 6 to Astor Pl

**Ike's Cafe**
- **Address**: 30 W 21st St, Manhattan (Flatiron)
- **Highlights**: Low-key, no-frills private karaoke rooms. Favored by locals for its unpretentious vibe and reasonable prices. Full bar and food menu.
- **Price Range**: $25–40/hour/room
- **Hours**: 7:00 PM–4:00 AM
- **Transport**: N, Q, R, W to 23rd St; F, M to 23rd St

**Rush Bar & Karaoke**
- **Address**: 328 5th Ave, Manhattan (Koreatown/Midtown)
- **Highlights**: Spacious Koreatown karaoke with modern rooms. Good for large groups and birthday parties. Full bar and Korean bar food.
- **Price Range**: $35–55/hour/room
- **Hours**: 12:00 PM–4:00 AM
- **Transport**: B, D, F, M, N, Q, R to 34th St–Herald Sq

### Stage Karaoke / Open Mic

**Sing Sing Karaoke Bar**
- **Address**: 150 Avenue A, Manhattan (East Village)
- **Highlights**: High-energy stage karaoke in a bar setting. Sing in front of a supportive (and usually tipsy) crowd. Classic dive bar atmosphere. The best open-mic karaoke in the East Village.
- **Price Range**: Free entry; drinks $8–15
- **Hours**: 2:00 PM–4:00 AM
- **Transport**: L to 1st Ave; 6 to Astor Pl

**Brady's Dance Asylum / Mona's**
- **Address**: 22 Avenue B, Manhattan (East Village)
- **Highlights**: Legendary dive bar karaoke. Starts late (around midnight), goes until 4 AM. Characters from all walks of life. The quintessential NYC karaoke dive experience.
- **Price Range**: Free entry; drinks $6–12
- **Hours**: 8:00 PM–4:00 AM
- **Transport**: L to 1st Ave; F to 2nd Ave

## Tips

- Koreatown (32nd St between 5th and 6th Ave) has the highest density of noraebang — walk in and compare prices
- BYOB is common at Koreatown karaoke (corkage fee ~$10–20/bottle)
- Stage karaoke at dive bars is free but you should buy drinks
- Weekends require reservations for private rooms; walk-ins may wait 1–2 hours
- Song selection at Koreatown spots is strongest in Korean and English; Japanese and Chinese songs available but smaller catalogs
