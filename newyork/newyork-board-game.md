# New York Board Game Guide

**Data Note**: This guide is compiled from publicly available information.

---

## Overview

New York City has a thriving board game scene centered around dedicated board game cafes and bars in Manhattan and Brooklyn. From strategy-heavy Euro games to social party games, NYC offers spaces for every type of player. The Unpub and PAX Unplugged conventions also bring the national board game community to the city annually.

## Recommended Venues

### Board Game Cafes & Bars

**The Unqua Quaffers / Brooklyn Strategist**
- **Address**: 333 5th Ave, Brooklyn (Park Slope)
- **Highlights**: One of NYC's most beloved board game destinations. Hundreds of games in the library, from Catan to Twilight Imperium. Offers food and drinks. Hosts tournaments, D&D nights, and learn-to-play events. Family-friendly during the day, adult-oriented in the evening.
- **Price Range**: $10–15 cover (includes game rental)
- **Transport**: R train to 25th St (Brooklyn)

**Hex & Company**
- **Address**: 314 Grand St, Manhattan (Lower East Side); 137 West 26th St (Chelsea)
- **Highlights**: NYC's premier board game cafe with two locations. Extensive game library curated by knowledgeable staff. Serves coffee, beer, wine, and light bites. Popular for social deduction games (Blood on the Clocktower, Secret Hitler). Good for both walk-ins and large groups.
- **Price Range**: $10 cover + food/drink minimum
- **Transport**: F, J, M, Z to Delancey-Essex St (LES); 1 to 28th St (Chelsea)

**Sip & Play**
- **Address**: 197A Stanton St, Manhattan (Lower East Side)
- **Highlights**: Cozy board game cafe with a curated library of 200+ games. Known for its welcoming atmosphere for newcomers. Serves specialty coffee, boba, and snacks. Regular events include board game nights and TTRPG one-shots.
- **Price Range**: $8–12 cover
- **Transport**: F, J, M, Z to Delancey-Essex St

**The Brooklyn Strategist (Cobble Hill)**
- **Address**: 333 Court St, Brooklyn (Cobble Hill)
- **Highlights**: Specializes in strategic and war games. Has a dedicated following among serious hobbyist gamers. Offers game rentals, tournaments, and a retail shop. Beer and wine available.
- **Price Range**: $10–15 cover
- **Transport**: F, G to Bergen St

### Board Game Bars

**Word Cafe / The Wayland**
- **Address**: 700 E 9th St, Manhattan (East Village)
- **Highlights**: While not exclusively a board game bar, this East Village spot has a solid collection of party games and a great cocktail program. Good for a casual game night with drinks.
- **Price Range**: Cocktails $14–18
- **Transport**: L to 1st Ave; 6 to Astor Pl

## Tips

- Most board game cafes charge a cover ($8–15) that includes unlimited game play
- Staff ("game gurus") can teach you any game in the library — don't be afraid to ask
- Blood on the Clocktower and social deduction games are extremely popular in NYC; check meetup.com for organized events
- Weekday evenings are best for a relaxed session; weekends get crowded
- Brooklyn (Park Slope, Cobble Hill) has the highest concentration of board game cafes
